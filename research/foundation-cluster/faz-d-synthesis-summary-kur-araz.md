## Evidence & Confidence Summary

### C-KÜR-001: Geographic Range & Settlement Density
- **Confidence:** STRONGLY_SUPPORTED (range confirmed; density variation important)
- **Faz C red-flag:** FALSE_PRECISION — "occupied" implies uniform presence
- **Production requirement:** Distinguish core region (central Kura valley, dense) vs. margins (Armenia, Iran, lighter)

### C-KÜR-002: Mentesh Tepe Genetic Admixture
- **Confidence:** ESTABLISHED (peer-reviewed Nature Communications)
- **Evidence:** Genome-wide analysis showing Anatolian farmer + Caucasus/Iranian admixture
- **Production note:** High-quality genetic evidence; no revision needed; sample size limited (single cemetery) but standard for aDNA studies

### C-KÜR-003: Earliest Wine Evidence (Gadachrili Gora)
- **Confidence:** ESTABLISHED (chemical residue analysis)
- **Evidence:** Delta13C/deltaD isotope values definitive for wine fermentation biomarkers
- **Dating:** ~6000 BCE confirmed
- **Production note:** Straightforward; note this is "earliest known" (pending discoveries)

### C-KÜR-004: 5th Millennium Gene Flow to Pontic Steppe
- **Confidence:** STRONGLY_SUPPORTED (genetic signal strong; mechanism ambiguous)
- **Faz C red-flag:** CLAIM_MUTATION_SUSPECTED — "diaspora" overstates coordinated movement
- **Production requirement:** Replace "diaspora" with "gene flow" or "expansion"; note mechanism (invasion vs. gradual migration vs. elite contact) remains debated

### C-KÜR-005: Ceramic Symbolism & Female Figurines ⚠️
- **Confidence:** PLAUSIBLE (design influence established; symbolism speculative)
- **Faz C red-flag:** SPECULATION_FLAGGED — figurine symbolic meaning is inferred, not demonstrated
- **Production requirement:** Separate established (ceramic design influence from Hassuna/Halaf) from unknown (figurine meaning—fertility goddess? household spirits? toys?)
