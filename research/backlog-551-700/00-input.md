### Scoring Methodology

Each candidate scored on six axes (high/medium/low):
- **evidence_density** — abundance of known sources
- **novelty** — originality vs. mainstream historiography
- **research_gap** — unexplored vs. well-trodden territory
- **counterintuitive_value** — challenges conventional narratives
- **narrative_tension** — dramatic/philosophical stakes
- **researchability** — likelihood of finding quality sources in Phase B

Risk assessment includes source fragmentation, interpretation hazards, and primary source availability.
