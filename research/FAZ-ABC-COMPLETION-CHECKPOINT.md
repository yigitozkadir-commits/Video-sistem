## ⚠️ Historiographic Tensions & Qualifications

### Major Paradigm Shifts Documented

1. **Kurgan Hypothesis (1950s Gimbutas) → Genetic Multi-Wave Model (2015+)**
   - Old: Indo-European "invasion" from Pontic-Caspian steppe
   - New: Multiple admixture waves; pastoral expansion gradual
   - Impact: KUR-ARAZ, KURG, ESKİ-HALK sections qualify claims

2. **Climate Determinism (Agricultural Revolution → Pastoral Societies) → Multi-Causal Model (2010+)**
   - Old: Environment → social structure (arid steppe = mobility → warfare)
   - New: Climate + technology + cultural choice + trade routes all contribute
   - Impact: BOZ-02A requires explicit reframing away from determinism

3. **Soviet Orientalism (Tengriism as "monolithic pre-Islamic religion") → Stratified Systems + Shamanism (1990-2020)**
   - Old: Tengri = state religion (top-down)
   - New: Tengri = elite ideology; shamanism = folk layer; Islam layers on top
   - Impact: MİT section requires source-gap transparency

4. **Heroic Historiography (Manas, Dede Korkut, Ryskulov) → Political Construction (post-Soviet)**
   - Old: Narratives as historical facts
   - New: Recognized as Soviet suppression → post-Soviet reconstruction → modern instrumentalization
   - Impact: MANAS, DEDE-KORKUT, RYSKULOV require explicit caveat language
