## Key Historiographic Debate

**Unresolved question:** Were Botai and Western steppe horses **single lineage** or **independent domestication events**?

- **School 1:** Botai sole origin; Western steppe horses descended (genetic continuity)
- **School 2:** Dual domestication; Western steppe proved superior and outcompeted Botai lineage
- **School 3 (emerging):** Botai local adaptation; Western steppe as separate innovation with genetic introgression

**Current consensus:** Multiple-domestication hypothesis gaining acceptance but not dominant
