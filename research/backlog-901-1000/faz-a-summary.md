### **CAND-901-950-A: "Asharianism and Naqshbandi Sufism"** 
**Backlog Range:** 901–950  
**Başlık:** Asharianism and Naqshbandi Sufism: Contested Theological Knowledge in the Ottoman Cosmos

| Metrik | Skor |
|--------|------|
| Evidence Density | **High** — Akademik tartışma, Ottoman theologians elyazması |
| Novelty | **High** — Türkçe/İngilizce akademisyenler bu sentezu açıkça tartışmadı |
| Research Gap | **High** — Ottoman ulema vs. Sufi lodge: neden çatışma? nasıl çözüldü? |
| Narrative Tension | **High** — "Reason vs. Mysticism" classic frame, ama Ottoman bağlamında nadir |
| Researchability | **High** — Hanafism, Maturidism, Naqshbandi order hepsi iyi belgelenmiş |

**Central Question:** How did Ottoman scholars reconcile rational Islamic theology (Asharianism/Maturidism debate) with experiential mysticism (Sufi orders)?

**Research Risk:** **Medium** — Ottoman theological manuscripts Turkish/Arabic gerekli; some Sufi lodge documents fragmentary. But academic historiography (ANU, Cambridge) zaten hazır.

**Production Angle:** "The Ottoman Theology Wars: How Scholars Chose Between Reason and Mysticism"

**Recommendation:** ✓ Ready for Faz B
